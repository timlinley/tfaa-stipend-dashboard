Privacy

TFAA Stipend Dashboard · Updated October 6, 2026

The TFAA Stipend Dashboard shows results from the Texas Fine Arts Administrators stipend survey. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Signing in with Google

When you sign in, Google tells the dashboard your name and email address. The dashboard uses your email address only to check whether you are on the list of people allowed to view the results. It does not store your sign-in, your name or your email address, and it does not ask for access to your Google Drive, Gmail, contacts or anything else in your account.

Your browser keeps your sign-in for the current tab so you do not have to sign in on every refresh. It is cleared when you close the tab.

Survey results

The results come from district responses to the TFAA stipend survey. The names and email addresses of the people who filled in the survey are never sent to the dashboard.

Sharing

Information from the dashboard is not sold or shared with advertisers. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard web server logs, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.
